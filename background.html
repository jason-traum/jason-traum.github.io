About me

My skills, my paintings and what I do outside work

Skills: quant and AI, product, and working with people

I grew up loving math and science and always figured I'd be a doctor. At Brown I switched to applied math and econ, then spent six years in finance trying to make our models more precise. The work of mine that got used the most was the data visualization palette I built, which became the Wealth Management standard, and the analytics package I automated, which still runs every month. Now I mostly try to build things people use every day.
