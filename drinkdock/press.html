About DrinkDock

Boilerplate for the end of a story or a release.

DrinkDock is designing age-verified vending machines for canned beer, seltzer and cocktails. Each machine checks ID on every alcohol sale, takes contactless payment and lowers the can to a lit pickup port, so simple orders at bars, hotels and venues don’t wait behind cocktails. Based in Philadelphia, DrinkDock is forming its first pilot with Philadelphia bars and hotels, with first installs planned for 2027.
