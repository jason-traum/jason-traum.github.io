Run your own numbers
What do you need from us for pricing?
Your venue and city. If you can, tell us your busiest nights and send a photo of where a machine might go, and we’ll include which model fits.
Who keeps the drink revenue?
You do. The machine sells your inventory under your license, and your venue is the merchant of record. Sales settle to your bank account nightly, minus card processing. DrinkDock never takes a cut of drink sales.
What happens at the end of a lease?
Renew, upgrade to a newer model, or we pick the machine up at no charge.
Can a beverage brand pay for the machine?
No. Pennsylvania doesn’t allow suppliers to give retailers equipment, so a brand can’t cover your lease. A brand can supply a point-of-sale wrap for the machine at no charge, within PLCB limits. How brands work with DrinkDock.
Is there an install fee?
Not on a lease. Purchases include install within 30 miles of Philadelphia.
