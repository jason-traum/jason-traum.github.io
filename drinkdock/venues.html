DrinkDock for arenas and event halls

At a venue, the whole crowd wants a drink in the same ten minutes. A row of DrinkDock Duos turns a stretch of wall into extra points of sale, with stand staff nearby to watch them. Indoor arenas, club levels, enclosed concourses and event halls.

Talk to usSee DrinkDock Duo

Built for the rush

Two guests at a time

Each Duo has two guest stations, each with its own screen, scanner, reader, lift and pickup port, so two sales don’t wait on one lift.

Drinks per minute

The design target is about 20 seconds per sale at each station, or roughly three sales a minute per station at best. We haven’t measured it on a production unit, so we’d measure completed drinks per minute with you during a real rush.

Concourses and club levels

Several side by side next to existing stands, or one on the club level as a second place to buy.

One view of every machine

Manager shows every machine’s sales and stock, so runners restock the right one before the break.

Works with your concessionaire

Machines sell under the venue or concessionaire’s license, with sales reporting by stand.

Planning for next season?

Tell us your venue, stands and event calendar. We’ll talk through what a pilot at one stand would look like.
