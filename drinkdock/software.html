      Sales and stock

      Revenue, drinks sold and time to serve by the hour, plus what’s left in each selection. Compare tonight with last Friday.

      Restock alerts

      Pick a threshold for each selection. When a row hits it, Manager texts whoever is on restock that night.

      Staff controls

      Pause the machine, lock out a guest’s ID for the night, reset a two-drink limit, or set an earlier cutoff than your license requires.

      Compliance log

      Every sale and every declined ID with a timestamp, so you have a record of each age check. No ID photos are stored.

      Menu and prices

      Swap a drink, change a price or set a happy hour price window. Changes go straight to the machine.

      Payouts and exports

      Nightly payouts to your bank account and CSV exports for your bookkeeper. Toast and Square integrations are on the roadmap.

      Every machine in one place

      The main bar and the rooftop, or three bars across town, side by side.

      Roles and access

      Owners see everything. Bar staff get pause, lockout and restock, and nothing else.
