      Built for whoever closes out the night

      Staff controls

      Pause the machine, block a guest’s ID until close from their sale, reset one guest’s 20-minute wait, or set an earlier last sale. The two-drink limit can’t be turned off.

      Stock and restock

      What’s left in each row, and a text to whoever is on restock when a row hits the threshold you set. Put a bestseller in several rows and the machine sells from them in turn.

      Sales

      Machine sales and drinks by the hour, compared with last week. These are the machine’s own sales, not a measure of what it added to the whole venue.

      Compliance log

      Every sale, every declined ID and every staff action, with a timestamp. No ID photos or images are stored.

      Menu and prices

      Swap a drink, change a price or set a happy hour window. Changes go straight to the machine.

      Payouts and exports

      Nightly payouts to your bank account and CSV exports for your bookkeeper. Toast and Square integrations are on the roadmap.

      Every machine in one place

      The main bar and the lobby bar, side by side.

      Roles and access

      Owners see everything. Bar staff get pause, block and restock, and nothing else.
