About DrinkDock

DrinkDock is designing age-verified vending machines for canned beer, seltzer and cocktails, so busy venues can sell simple drinks without making every order wait for a bartender. We’re based in Philadelphia and forming our first pilot here.

Why we started

We started DrinkDock after too many Friday nights stuck in a bar line for a drink that takes five seconds to open.

Most people have a limit on how long they’ll wait for a drink. Past it, some order something simpler, some come back later and some leave. The ones who leave are sales the bar never sees, and they leave on the nights that matter most.

How we build it

Bartenders keep the drinks that need them

The machine takes canned orders so bartenders can spend their time on cocktails and guests.

Every alcohol sale is checked

ID on every alcohol sale, two drinks per scan, and staff who can stop the machine at any time.

We’ll install and support pilot machines ourselves

The team you talk to is the team that installs and supports pilot machines, starting in Philadelphia.

You see what it sold

Manager shows what the machine sold, when, and what it declined, so you can judge it on your own numbers.
