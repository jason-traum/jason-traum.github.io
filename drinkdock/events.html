DrinkDock for events and catering

Rent a machine for a reception, festival or launch. It handles the beer and seltzer so your bar team can run wine and cocktails, and every alcohol sale is ID-checked.

Check a dateGet an event quote

How an event rental works

1. Pick a date and a machine

One or Compact for most rooms, Duo for large crowds. We quote per machine per day.

2. Set the menu

You or your caterer choose the drinks and prices. We load an event menu screen, with your logo if you like.

3. We deliver and set up

Within 30 miles of Philadelphia, before your doors open. A technician is on call during the event.

4. Pickup and report

We collect the machine afterward and send a sales report by drink and by hour.

Alcohol at an event is sold under the venue’s or licensed caterer’s license. We coordinate with them before the date.

Good fits

Weddings and receptions

Guests grab a beer without waiting behind a signature-cocktail line.

Festivals and block parties

Extra points of sale at the busiest hours, with ID on every sale.

Brand activations

A wrapped machine stocked with one brand’s lineup, with sales data after.

Have a date in mind?

Tell us the date, the venue and the headcount. We’ll confirm availability within two business days.
