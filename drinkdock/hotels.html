DrinkDock for hotels and resorts

Pool decks, rooftops and lobby bars often run with one bartender and a line. A DrinkDock serves the canned beer, seltzer and cocktails, so your bartender keeps up with everything else.

Where it earns its place

Pool decks and rooftops

Afternoon rushes with one bartender. Every alcohol sale is ID-checked, which matters at a pool with families.

Lobby bars

Covers the rush when a conference lets out or a wedding party arrives, without scheduling a second bartender for an hour.

Receptions and events

A machine stocked for the event handles beer and seltzer while your banquet bartender runs wine and cocktails.

Hours you already have

Sales lock to the hours on your license, and you can set tighter hours per machine, like pool hours only.

What hotel teams ask first

Can guests charge it to their room?
Not yet. Room charge through your property management system is on our roadmap. For now guests pay by card or phone.
Does it need its own license?
The machine sells under your existing hotel license, from your inventory. Before any install we request a written advisory opinion from the PLCB for your setup. See compliance.
Who restocks it?
Whoever restocks your bar today. It takes about five minutes, and Manager texts them when a row runs low.
Will it look out of place?
The cabinet is matte graphite with brushed metal trim, and the screen shows your drinks and prices, not ads. Custom wraps in your property’s colors are available.

Run F&B at a Philadelphia hotel?

Tell us about your outlets and your busiest hours. We’ll recommend a machine for each.
