DrinkDock for bars and nightlife

On your best nights, every order waits for the same bartenders. A DrinkDock at the end of the bar takes the canned orders, so the cocktail line moves and a guest who only wants a beer doesn’t have to wait for one.

See if it fits your barRun your numbers

Where it goes and what it sells

For most bars the right size is a DrinkDock One: 24 rows on a 36 by 30 inch footprint, plus room in front for a guest to stand.

Placement

At the end of the bar, against a wall, inside your bartenders’ line of sight, on one standard outlet. Placement plan.

Menu

Your top canned and bottled drinks: light beer, IPAs, hard seltzer, canned cocktails, cider and non-alcoholic options.

What stays the same

Your distributor, your prices, your staff and your license. Draft, cocktails and shots stay at the bar.

What you see

Machine sales by the hour in Manager. Telling how much of that is new, rather than moved from the bar, takes comparing similar nights, which we’d plan with you.

How the work splits on a busy night

It gives canned orders their own place to buy. Everything else stays with your team.

AreaAt the machineAt the bar

The bars it fits best

Volume bars

Busy Thursday through Saturday, with a line at the bar for a couple of hours a night.

Sports bars

Game days put the whole room at the bar at halftime and between innings.

College and neighborhood bars

Where cans and seltzers are already a big share of what you sell.

Music venues and clubs

Where guests don’t want to miss the set to wait for a beer.
