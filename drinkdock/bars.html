DrinkDock for bars and nightlife

On your best nights, your bartenders are the bottleneck. A DrinkDock at the end of the bar takes the canned orders, so the line moves and the guest who was about to give up buys a drink.

Book a demoEstimate your lost sales

Where it goes and what it sells

For most bars the right size is a DrinkDock One: 24 selections in about three feet of floor.

Placement

At the end of the bar or near the dance floor, inside your bartenders’ line of sight, on one standard outlet.

Menu

Your top canned and bottled drinks: light beer, IPAs, hard seltzer, canned cocktails, cider and non-alcoholic options.

What stays the same

Your distributor, your prices, your staff and your license. Draft, cocktails and shots stay at the bar.

What you see

Machine sales by the hour next to your busiest hours in Manager, so you can see what it adds at the peak.

The bars it fits best

Volume bars

Busy Thursday through Saturday, with a line at the bar for a couple of hours a night.

Sports bars

Game days put the whole room at the bar at halftime and between innings.

College and neighborhood bars

Where cans and seltzers are already a big share of what you sell.

Music venues and clubs

Where guests don’t want to miss the set to wait for a beer.
