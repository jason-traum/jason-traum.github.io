Beer vending machine for bars, hotels and venues

Keep canned orders moving while your bartenders make cocktails

DrinkDock is a glass-front vending machine for canned beer, seltzer and cocktails. Guests scan their ID, tap to pay and take a cold can from the pickup port, so simple orders don’t wait behind mixed drinks.

At 11 on a Friday, a can of seltzer waits behind every espresso martini

At a crowded bar, every order waits in the same line however long it takes to make. Some guests who only want a beer wait, some order something simpler, and some give up. In a 2024 survey, 61% of U.S. consumers said they had left a line before reaching their turn.

Source: Waitwhile, survey of 1,000 U.S. consumers, 2024

Where it goes

At the end of the bar, against a wall, inside your bartenders’ line of sight. A DrinkDock One needs a 36 by 30 inch footprint, 48 inches of clear floor in front for guests, and a standard outlet.

Questions operators ask first

All questions

Is this legal in Pennsylvania?
The machine sells under your license, from your inventory, and you stay responsible for every sale. The PLCB hasn’t issued an opinion on DrinkDock yet. For each pilot venue we request a written advisory opinion describing that venue’s exact setup, and we don’t install without a clear answer. More on compliance.
Who restocks it?
Your team, with drinks from your distributor, at your prices. Manager texts whoever is on restock when a row reaches the threshold you set.
What if it breaks on a Saturday night?
A jammed row switches itself off and the rest of the machine keeps selling. If a can doesn’t reach the port, the guest isn’t charged. During the pilot, one of our team is on call by phone through your service hours. Details.
What does it cost?
It depends on the machine and the term. Ask for the pricing sheet and we’ll send it with a note on which machine fits your space.
