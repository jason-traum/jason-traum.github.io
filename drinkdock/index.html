The beer vending machine built for busy bars

Sell canned drinks at the peak without adding staff

DrinkDock sits at the end of your bar and sells canned beer, seltzer and cocktails on its own. Guests scan their ID, tap to pay and pick up a cold can in about 20 seconds, while your bartenders keep making the drinks that need them.

At a crowded bar every order waits in the same line, however long it takes to make. Guests who only want a beer wait, order something simpler, or give up. In a 2024 survey, 61% of U.S. consumers said they had left a line before reaching their turn.

How a sale works

About 20 seconds, start to finish. Nobody behind the bar stops what they’re doing.

More on how it works

  1. 0:00Pick a drink

    Guests choose up to two drinks on the touchscreen. You set the menu and the prices.

  2. 0:05Scan an ID

    The scanner reads the barcode on a driver’s license or state ID and checks it before payment opens.

  3. 0:10Tap to pay

    Contactless cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay.

  4. 0:15Pick it up

    An internal lift carries the can down to the pickup port, so nothing drops or foams over.

What changes behind the bar

Bartenders stay on cocktails

Canned orders move to the machine, so the person making a $14 margarita isn’t also opening seltzers.

No one added to the schedule

The machine runs itself. A barback restocks it in about five minutes, and DrinkDock Manager texts them before a row runs out.

Sales you’re losing now

Guests who would have left the line buy from the machine instead.

Built around Pennsylvania’s liquor rules
